# Directory listings & backlink kit

Why: Brewstamp ranks on page 1 for most head terms (avg pos ~9) but rarely in
the top 5, and the USA — the biggest impression market — converts at 0.7% CTR.
On-page work is largely done; the remaining lever is authority (links from
relevant, trusted sites). See `docs/seo-snapshots.md` (2026-10-21).

Ready-to-paste copy is below. Keep it in sync with `src/lib/plans.ts` if prices
or the free allowance change (free allowance = `FREE_STAMPS`, currently 50).

## Targets, in order

| # | Where | Why | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Your own customers' websites** | Most relevant links possible (cafe → cafe loyalty tool) | Ask paying shops to add "Loyalty card by Brewstamp" linking to brewstamp.app on their site/footer or "Rewards" page. 14 paying + engaged trials. |

| 6 | **Coffee trade press** | Topical authority | Guest article or customer story: Perfect Daily Grind, Sprudge, Barista Magazine (US), Café Culture (UK), BeanScene. Angle: "what 8 stamps for a free coffee does to visit frequency" with real (anonymised) data. Check `project_outreach_may16` memory — don't re-pitch the same 6 outlets without checking replies. |

Skip: paid link schemes, generic "submit your site" directories, and app
marketplaces that need an integration we don't have (e.g. Square App
Marketplace).

## Copy

**Name:** Brewstamp
**Website:** https://brewstamp.app
**Category:** Customer loyalty / loyalty program software (cafes, coffee shops)
**Pricing:** Free for the first 50 stamps · Pro US$12/mo · Business US$29/mo (annual = two months free)

**Tagline (≤60 chars):**
Digital coffee loyalty cards — no app for customers

**Short description (≤160 chars):**
A digital stamp card for cafes. Customers scan a QR code — no app, no signup — and collect stamps toward a free coffee. Free to start.

**Long description:**
Brewstamp replaces the paper coffee punch card with a digital one that
customers never have to download. They scan the QR code at your counter,
request a stamp in their browser, and your staff approve it on any phone,
tablet or computer in real time. After the number of stamps you choose, they
get a free coffee.

- No customer app and no signup — the card lives in the browser, with an
  optional Apple or Google Wallet pass
- No POS integration needed: works alongside any till
- Real-time stamp approvals, so no stamp fraud
- Customer list and visit insights you never had with paper
- One staff login on Pro; up to 3 shops, unlimited staff logins and CSV exports on Business (US$29/month)
- Available in 14 languages
- Free for your first 50 stamps; Pro is US$12/month for unlimited stamps

**Features list (for checkbox-style forms):** digital stamp card, QR code check-in, Apple Wallet, Google Wallet, real-time approvals, rewards management, customer database, analytics, multi-location, staff accounts, CSV export, multi-language.

**Screenshots:** reuse the product images on the homepage and `/features`.
